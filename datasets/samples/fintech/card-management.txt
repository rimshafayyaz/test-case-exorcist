Test case: Activate a new debit card
Preconditions:
- A customer account exists with a new, inactive debit card.
1. Log in to the mobile banking app with the customer's username and password.
   Expected: The Accounts overview screen is displayed.
2. Open the Cards screen, select the new card and tap Activate.
   Expected: The card status changes to Active.
3. Enter the last four digits of the card.
4. Set a new 4-digit PIN.
   Expected: The message "PIN set successfully" is displayed.

Test case: Freeze and unfreeze a card
1. Make sure the card is active.
2. Open the Cards screen.
   Expected: The list of cards is displayed.
3. If the card is not frozen, tap Freeze card.
   Expected: The card status shows Frozen.
4. Tap Unfreeze card.
   - Verify that card payments work again.

Test case: Report a lost card
Preconditions:
- The customer is logged in.
1. Open the Cards screen.
   Expected: The list of cards is displayed.
2. Tap Report lost card quickly.
   Expected: A confirmation dialog appears.
3. Tap Confirm.
   Expected: Click OK to close the dialog.

Test case: View the card PIN
Preconditions:
- The customer is logged in.
- The card is active.
1. Open the Cards screen.
   Expected: The list of cards is displayed.
2. Tap Show PIN on the active card.
   Expected: A one-time password is sent by SMS.
3. Enter the one-time password from the SMS.
   Expected: The 4-digit PIN is displayed for 10 seconds.
